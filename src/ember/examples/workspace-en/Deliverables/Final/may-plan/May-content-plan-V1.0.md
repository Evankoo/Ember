Example confirmation: on May 10 the fictional client confirmed this version. Names, content and confirmation are all fictional.

## Overall direction
Three posts tied together by “leaving a little room in an ordinary day”. Following the gentle tone of the Chenyu brand brief, each post gives only one reason to visit and does not pile up product information. This plan is a confirmed sample; later tasks can refer to this version, and the original May record stays complete.

## One: A cup for the morning
Title: Before heading out, keep three minutes for yourself. Open with three actions: pushing the door, taking the cup and walking on down the street. The body introduces the usual choices that are easy to take away, without promising how fast a cup is ready. Shoot steam from the cup, the counter handover and steps at the door. Closing: “Next time you pass, give your morning a small pause.”

## Two: Sit a while in the afternoon
Title: Read today's page slowly. Open with a book lying open by the window and describe the cup, the light and the quiet stay. Shoot the window table, the pages and cup details, with no customer's face in frame. The closing reminds that seats depend on the day, without promising a free table.

## Three: How to order the first time
Title: Start from the taste you like. Explain ordering in three directions, fresh, rich and no coffee, with product close-ups and simple text cards. Closing: “Tell the barista the taste you like and choose a cup together.”
