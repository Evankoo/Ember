## B: Monthly content plan
Example base price: 3,800. Delivers six content directions, a monthly order suggestion and a shot list; includes two rounds of collected revisions. The plan does not include shooting, editing, publishing or advertising; any extra needs have their scope confirmed separately first.

## Timing and feedback
The example pace for a first draft is five working days after complete materials and the request are confirmed. In each revision round the client collects feedback once, changing only the agreed content; new topics, quantities or scope need a new confirmation. Feedback dates and the final delivery date are filled in for each collaboration.
