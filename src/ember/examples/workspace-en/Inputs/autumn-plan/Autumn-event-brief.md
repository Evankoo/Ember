# Autumn Event Brief

Fictional example material · Input · 2026-09-12 · V1.0
The client, event, dates and arrangements in this brief are fictional.

## Goal of the new round
Chenyu Coffee plans an in-store event, “A Small Autumn Pause”, from September 26 to October 2, alongside two seasonal products. It wants to keep the quiet, friendly feel of the May posts, so readers first understand what moments the new products suit and then decide whether to visit.

## Event idea
Visitors can pick up an autumn note card, write something they want to do more slowly lately, and put the card on a display shelf in the café. Taking part needs no personal information and does not require sharing on social media. The price and daily number of the two-person set are still to be checked with the client, so the copy does not mention a discount amount yet.

## Content requirements
Prepare three posts: one introducing the new products, one explaining how the note cards work, and one showing an everyday scene with a friend. Titles, openings and closings keep the tone of the May plan; do not just swap autumn words into the May text. Each post comes with shot suggestions and clearly marks the information still to be confirmed.

## What we have and what is missing
We have the autumn theme, the event dates and the product descriptions. Missing are the final price list, quantities, the finished note card and data after the event. This round first produces a draft that can be checked; after the client confirms, the final version and publishing material follow.

## Later observation
The review is meant to look at orders of the new products, in-store participation and note card feedback. These are only metrics to collect; there is no actual result data yet, and none can be written as an achievement in advance.
