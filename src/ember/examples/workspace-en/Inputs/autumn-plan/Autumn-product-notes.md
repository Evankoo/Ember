# Autumn Product Notes

Fictional example material · Input · 2026-09-13 · V1.0
Product names, descriptions, supply plans and numbers are all fictional.

## Apple oat latte
The idea is to bring the freshness of apple and the softness of oats into an autumn hot drink. The content focuses on taking a warm cup, without describing unverified health benefits. Keep the cup and apple slices in the shot with natural light; do not add decorations for the picture that the real recipe does not have. It is planned as a hot drink; the final recipe and price are to be confirmed by the client.

## Osmanthus black tea scone
The idea pairs black tea and a light osmanthus aroma with coffee, showing the texture when broken and a table set with one cup and one scone. The body can say “keep a little more time for the afternoon”, without taste guarantees or sales rankings. Whether it is sold on its own, the daily quantity and the ingredient labels still need checking.

## Shared shot suggestions
First shoot both products in full, then a hand holding a cup, a scone being broken and two friends sitting opposite each other. Keep background props simple, with the main colors coming from the wooden table, paper cards and the food itself. All material is prepared only for this fictional example and does not stand for real products or a real café.

## Information usable in copy
Usable: the provisional names of the two products, the autumn theme and the scenes of a warm drink and an afternoon pairing. Not yet: price, limited quantities, discounts and the final recipe. The related fields in the draft stay “to be confirmed”, and a planned supply must never be written as already on sale.

## Relation to the event
The product introduction is one way into the autumn note card event: first the everyday experience, then one sentence about the event. How to take part follows the autumn event brief.
