# May Content Request

Fictional example material · Input · 2026-05-06 · V1.0
The names, requests and arrangements in this document are fictional.

## Goal of this round
Prepare three May posts for Chenyu Coffee so that someone seeing them for the first time knows what kind of day the café is good for. May has no new products and no time-limited discounts; the posts find their angle in the coffee already served and in scenes inside the café.

## Three observations
The first post is about taking a coffee in the morning: film walking in, taking the cup and carrying on; it can open with “Before heading out, keep three minutes for yourself.” The second is about an afternoon seat: show a book, a cup and the changing light by the window to give a sense of staying, without promising a seat is always free. The third is about ordering for the first time: explain the fresh, the rich and the no-coffee choices clearly to help readers find their taste.

## Deliverables and timing
Each post needs a title, opening, body direction, shot list and closing suggestion. The plan is checked before May 10, and the example publishing dates are May 12, 20 and 28. Only after the whole plan is confirmed is it used to prepare publishing.

## Details to check
Keep the plain tone of the brand brief, with no rankings or guarantees. Check the drink names, opening hours and the price board in the photos one by one; describe the location only as a neighborhood scene, with no exact address. When confirming, the client first answers: are the three themes right, is the tone right, can the existing material be shot.
