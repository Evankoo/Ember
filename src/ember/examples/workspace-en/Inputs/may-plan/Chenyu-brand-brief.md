# Chenyu Brand Brief

Fictional example material · Input · 2026-05-04 · V1.0
The studio, client, scenes and requirements in this document are fictional.

## Brand and customers
Chenyu Coffee is a fictional neighborhood café that wants commuters and nearby residents to pause for a moment in a busy day. Its main customers take coffee to go in the morning, bring a book in the afternoon, or meet friends at the weekend. Content starts from these concrete moments and never turns the café into a faraway travel destination.

## Tone and images
The writing is relaxed and plain, like a barista describing today's choices. Talk about the taste in the cup, the light by the window and the small things of walking in; avoid exaggerated adjectives, commanding slogans and unverified rankings. Photos use natural light and real use: products, seats and people's movements each take a share, and no customer can be recognized.

## This collaboration
May first sets a steady way of speaking: the three pieces answer what to drink, when to come and how to order. Each piece has one point and ends with a gentle suggestion. Store arrangements, prices and availability are confirmed by the fictional client before publishing; unconfirmed information stays in the draft.

## Background for later rounds
Keep the idea of “making an ordinary day a little more comfortable”; seasons change only the theme and material, never switching suddenly to loud promotions. This brief is shared by the May plan and the new autumn round.
